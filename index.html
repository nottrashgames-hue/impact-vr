IMPACT

VR striking simulator

Desktop controls

  • Click punch at the cursor (alternating hands)
  • Drag look around
  • WASD move
  • M menu (settings, gore, debug)
  • G gloves on / off
  • O reset opponent
  • F clean floor
  • R recenter
  • H performance HUD
  • B hitboxes & zones (hover to probe)

In VR

  • Punch with controllers or closed fists
  • Palm up (left hand) wrist menu · X pin it
  • Trigger (right) or index finger poke: press menu buttons
  • Grip / pinch at the rack: take or hang up gloves
  • Left stick walk · Right stick turn · Stick click recenter
  • B match the opponent's height (stand up straight)
  • A reset after a knockout
  • Y performance HUD

Contains simulated violence and blood.
